Our story

SHOQAN is a classic menswear concept built around a simple idea: a considered wardrobe makes everyday dressing effortless. Clean tailoring, quiet colors and versatile pieces form the foundation of our collection.

Inspired by the pace and character of Astana, we bring together suits, shirts and outerwear that feel at home at work and beyond. Each piece is chosen to complement the next, with an emphasis on proportion, comfort and enduring style.
